The "temporary placeholder" is finally gone, after 6 months of me procrastinating. This is the new karim.one.

## Stack
- [Astro](https://astro.build) for static pages, with React only where it's needed
- [Gradient ASCII](https://reactbits.dev/backgrounds/gradient-ascii) from React Bits for the landing page background
- [Motion](https://motion.dev) for the nav and buttons

Move your mouse over the background on the landing page. The characters scramble.
